# SOP-005 High-amount and out-of-pattern purchases

## §1 Definition
A purchase is high-amount when it is $500 or more, and out of pattern when it is at least
5 times the card's average purchase amount or larger than any purchase on the card in the
records available. Both are indicators, not proof: customers do make occasional large
purchases.

## §2 Checks
Record the card's average and largest previous purchase from the case data. Check whether the
merchant category is one the customer has used before in the available history. A large
purchase in a category the customer uses regularly is weaker evidence than a large purchase in
a new category combined with other indicators.

## §3 Escalation
Cases where the alerted purchase or the 24-hour spend is $1,000 or more require a second
review by a senior investigator before a confirmed-fraud disposition (see SOP-010 §3).
