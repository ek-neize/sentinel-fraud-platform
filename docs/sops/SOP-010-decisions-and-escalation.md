# SOP-010 Dispositions, escalation and decision authority

## §1 Decision authority
Only an investigator can decide a disposition or an action on a card. Models, rules and the
AI assistant provide information and suggestions; they never make or record a decision.
Tool outputs must not be phrased or copied into the case as conclusions.

## §2 Disposition codes
`confirmed_fraud`: the customer denied the transaction, or the facts leave no reasonable
doubt. `cleared`: reviewed and found genuine (false positive). `escalated`: passed to a senior
investigator or a specialist team. `pending`: waiting for information, with a note of what.

## §3 Escalation matrix
Escalate to a senior investigator when the alerted purchase or the 24-hour spend on the card
is $1,000 or more, when more than one card is linked to the same pattern, or when the facts
conflict. Escalate to the merchant-risk team when several unrelated cards show fraud at the
same merchant.

## §4 Actions on the card
Blocking or reissuing a card is an investigator decision recorded with its reason. A temporary
hold may be used under SOP-008 §4. Actions must be proportionate to the facts.
