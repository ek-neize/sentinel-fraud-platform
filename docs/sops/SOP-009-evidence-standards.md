# SOP-009 Evidence standards and case documentation

## §1 Facts versus signals
Case notes keep two lists apart. Verified facts are recorded data: transaction records,
watchlist status at the time, prior case dispositions, and customer or merchant responses.
Model signals are outputs of analytical tools: scores, risk bands, reason codes and any
AI-generated summary. Signals guide where to look; only facts support a disposition.

## §2 Verified evidence
Evidence must come from a record that existed at the time it is used and must be cited (record
type and ID or date). Statements that cannot be traced to a record are opinions and must be
labelled as the investigator's assessment.

## §3 Documentation template
Each case note contains: summary (one or two sentences), verified facts, model signals,
procedures followed (with SOP section references), open questions, disposition and the
investigator's name. AI-assisted drafts must be reviewed and edited by the investigator before
they are saved.

## §4 Privacy
Record only what the investigation needs. Do not record protected characteristics such as
gender, age, ethnicity or religion in case notes (see SOP-011).
