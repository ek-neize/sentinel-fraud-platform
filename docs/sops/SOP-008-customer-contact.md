# SOP-008 Customer contact and verification

## §1 When to contact
Contact the customer when the decision depends on whether the customer made the purchase:
CNP alerts above $500, bursts, and any case where the investigator is considering a card
block. Do not contact the customer for alerts that are being closed as false positives on
the records alone.

## §2 How to contact
Use only the contact details registered on the account. Ask the customer to confirm or deny
the specific transactions (date, merchant, amount). Never ask for the full card number, PIN,
security code or online banking passwords.

## §3 Recording outcomes
Record the date and time of contact, the channel, and the customer's answer for each
transaction as a verified fact. A customer's denial of a transaction is the strongest evidence
for confirmed fraud.

## §4 Customer unreachable
If the customer cannot be reached within 24 hours and the case involves a burst or a hot-list
hit, the investigator may place a temporary hold on the card and must record the reason. The
hold is reviewed when the customer responds.
