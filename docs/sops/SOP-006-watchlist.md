# SOP-006 Watchlist hits

## §1 Card hot-list
A card is placed on the hot-list shortly after a fraud report is received on it, and stays
listed for 14 days. A hot-list hit means the card was already linked to a reported fraud when
this transaction happened. It is a recorded fact about the card's status, and a strong
indicator, but it does not by itself confirm that this transaction is fraudulent.

## §2 Merchant risk list
Merchants are listed when their recent fraud rate is far above the overall rate. A merchant
risk-list hit is weak on its own: most transactions at listed merchants are genuine. Use it
together with card-level indicators.

## §3 List errors
Watchlists contain mistakes: entries are sometimes added from unverified external sources.
Before relying on a hit, check the listing reason and date where available. If the listing
cannot be explained, note this in the case and do not treat the hit as evidence.

## §4 Linked cases
When the hot-list hit relates to an earlier case on the same card, reference that case and its
disposition in the case file. Only dispositions that were recorded before the current alert
may be used.
